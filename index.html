Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding technology, privacy, and store integration.

How does Aurelune AI analyze skin without saving user photos?

The Aurelune engine operates directly in transient device memory (RAM) via WebAssembly. Camera frames are converted into numerical matrix vectors (evaluating surface light reflection, redness, and contrast) and the image is immediately discarded. No facial photos ever hit our database or third-party cloud disks.

Is Aurelune AI considered a medical diagnostic device?

No. Aurelune provides purely cosmetic, non-diagnostic hydration and routine recommendations under FDA MoCRA and 21 U.S.C. § 361. It is not designed to diagnose, cure, or mitigate medical skin conditions such as eczema, psoriasis, or melanoma.

How do the PubMed citations work?

Every product recommendation links to authentic, peer-reviewed clinical studies indexed on the National Library of Medicine (PubMed). Customers can inspect the exact clinical trial, study methodology, and biochemical mechanism supporting why an ingredient is recommended for their skin profile.

How hard is it to embed into our Shopify store?

Integration takes under 15 minutes. You paste a single lightweight JavaScript snippet into your store theme, map your product SKUs to ingredients in our dashboard, and the AI selfie scanner becomes active on your product pages or quiz funnels.

Aurelune Regulatory & Biometric Policy

1. FDA MoCRA & 21 U.S.C. § 361 Non-Diagnostic Boundary

Aurelune AI is a software recommendation engine intended strictly for cosmetic skin appearance evaluation. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatments for diseases such as rosacea, severe cystic acne, eczema, or skin cancers.

2. Illinois BIPA (740 ILCS 14/) & Biometric Data Handling

Aurelune does not store, sell, lease, or monetize customer biometric identifiers. All video frames captured through front-facing cameras are converted in transient client memory (RAM) into ephemeral mathematical vectors and immediately purged upon session completion.

3. Peer-Reviewed Ingredient Citations

Scientific citations from the National Library of Medicine (PubMed) are provided for cosmetic ingredient education. Finished consumer cosmetic products may vary depending on concentration, delivery vehicles, and skin compatibility.
